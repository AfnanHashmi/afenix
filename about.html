ABOUT AFENIX

We build the intelligence of tomorrow.

Afenix Technologies is a private-AI company built on one conviction: the most powerful intelligence a business can have is the one it actually owns.

Most AI sends your data somewhere else. We refuse that trade. AfenixCore is fine-tuned on your knowledge and deployed on your infrastructure — so the intelligence stays yours, end to end.

Mission

To give every organisation a private intelligence layer as capable as the frontier models — without surrendering a single byte of proprietary data to a third party.

Sovereignty and capability should not be a choice. AfenixCore is our answer: nine specialised agents, one private model, zero data leaving your walls.

Principles

Sovereign by design. Privacy is architecture, not a setting. No outbound calls, no telemetry, no copies of your weights.

Designed to feel different. Minimal, elegant, powerful. The interface disappears and leaves only the work.

Open foundations. Built on Llama 3.1, LangChain, ChromaDB, and Docker. Audit every layer. Never get locked in.

Approach

We treat each deployment as a craft, not a configuration. Your model learns your SOPs, your tone, your edge cases — then runs entirely on hardware you control.

The result is an AI that knows your business intimately and answers to no one but you.

Where we are

Afenix is preparing to launch, working closely with a small group of early partners across FinTech, B2B SaaS, and e-commerce to shape the platform before it opens broadly.

If that sounds like your team, we'd love to talk.
