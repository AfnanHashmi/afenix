The Origin

Why We Started

Every time a business uses a commercial AI tool, it feeds sensitive data into someone else's model. That is not intelligence. That is exposure.

We started Afenix Technologies to flip this entirely. We build private, fine-tuned large language models that live on your infrastructure, trained on your data, with zero dependency on external servers.

AfenixCore is our flagship platform — a corporate AI where every feature is powered by a model that knows your business and belongs to your business.
