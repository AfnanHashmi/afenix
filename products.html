FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does AfenixCore send any data outside our servers?
No. Zero outbound calls. The model, embeddings, and conversations all stay on your infrastructure — enforced at the network layer, not just by policy.
What hardware do we need?
A single GPU server handles Startup workloads. Corporate and Enterprise run on multi-GPU clusters we help you provision — on-prem or in your private cloud.
How long does deployment take?
Startup: 2–3 weeks. Corporate: 4–6 weeks. Enterprise: tailored to your compliance needs, typically 8–12 weeks.
Can we add custom agents?
Yes — included at Enterprise tier, available as add-ons for Corporate. The framework is built on CrewAI, so your engineers can extend it too.
What if we want to switch models later?
Because we use open weights, you can swap base models without rebuilding. Your data, embeddings, and fine-tuning datasets remain portable.
